# Product Notes

## Core Problem

Historical hobbyists, researchers, and tabletop wargamers often own large amounts of digital and physical reference material spread across disks, cloud folders, archives, shelves, and creator portals.

The app should answer:

- What do I have?
- What topic, conflict, period, force, battle, or theatre is it about?
- Which creator, publisher, campaign, or source produced it?
- Is it part of a package or a standalone item?
- Where is my copy?
- Have I printed, read, used, archived, or misplaced it?

## Design Principle

Separate shared catalog knowledge from personal library ownership.

Shared catalog records describe the thing. Personal library records describe whether a specific user owns it, where they keep it, and what private notes/status they attach to it.

## Stage 1

Stage 1 is catalog and location management only. It should not attempt to become a storage product.

## Stage 2 Candidates

- Optional managed uploads.
- Folder scanning and checksum matching.
- ISBN lookup.
- Document text extraction.
- STL previews and thumbnails.
- Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or NAS integrations.
- Semantic search.
